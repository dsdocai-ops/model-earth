How a session runs.

  1. Roll callThe chair calls each country. Answer "present" or "present and voting".
  2. Speakers listDelegates give short opening speeches on their country's position.
  3. Moderated caucusQuick, focused speeches on one part of the topic.
  4. Unmoderated caucusLeave your seat, find allies and start writing.
  5. Draft resolutionsBlocs present their papers and take questions.
  6. VotingAmendments first, then the resolutions themselves.

What a resolution looks like.

Every resolution has two parts: clauses that explain why, and clauses that say what to do.

Draft Resolution 1.1Committee: to be announcedSponsors: Brazil, Kenya, Norway

The General Assembly,

Recognizing that access to clean water is essential to public health,

Concerned by the number of communities without safe drinking water,

  1. Calls upon member states to share water-treatment technology;
  2. Encourages regional partnerships to fund new infrastructure;
  3. Decides to remain actively seized of the matter.

Points and motions to know.

Point of personal privilege

Something is affecting you, like the room being too cold.

"Point of personal privilege."

Point of order

The chair or a delegate made a procedural mistake.

"Point of order."

Point of inquiry

You have a question about the rules.

"Point of parliamentary inquiry."

Motion for a moderated caucus

Propose a focused debate with a topic, total time and speaking time.

"Motion for a 10-minute moderated caucus on funding, 1-minute speaking time."
