What is ARCMUN?

One day. One room. The whole planet.

Model UN puts students in the seats of real diplomats. Each delegate represents a country, researches where it stands, and negotiates with everyone else to write a resolution that can win a vote. At ARCMUN, hosted by Folsom High School, you get the full experience in a single day.

How it works

  1. 1

    Get your country

    Your school registers and you're assigned a country and a committee.

  2. 2

    Do your homework

    Research your country's position on the committee topic before the conference.

  3. 3

    Debate and deal

    Give speeches, caucus with allies, and win over the delegates who disagree.

  4. 4

    Pass a resolution

    Draft a resolution as a bloc, defend it, and bring it to a vote.
