Order of the day

Session times will be confirmed with the final programme.

  1. Registration and check-inDelegates collect credentials and placards.
  2. Opening ceremonyRemarks from the Secretariat and formal opening of the conference.
  3. First committee sessionOpening speeches, caucusing and working papers.
  4. Luncheon recessThe committees stand in recess.
  5. Second committee sessionDraft resolutions, amendments and voting procedure.
  6. Closing ceremony and awardsRecognition of outstanding delegates and close of the conference.
