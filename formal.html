About the conference

Diplomacy, practised in a single day.

Model United Nations places students in the role of national delegates. Each delegate researches the policy of an assigned member state and works with others in committee to draft, debate and adopt resolutions. ARCMUN 2027, hosted by Folsom High School, brings that experience to Folsom for one full day of committee sessions.

The delegate’s path

  1. I

    Country assignment

    Schools register their delegations and each delegate is assigned a member state and committee.

  2. II

    Research

    Delegates prepare their country’s position on the committee’s agenda in advance.

  3. III

    Committee debate

    Formal speeches, moderated and unmoderated caucuses, and negotiation between blocs.

  4. IV

    Resolution

    Draft resolutions are amended, debated and brought to a vote by the committee.
